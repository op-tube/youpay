The Half-Trillion Dollar Ad Machine

In 2024, Meta (Facebook, Instagram) generated over $164 billion in revenue, almost entirely from advertising. Meanwhile, Alphabet (Google, YouTube) generated nearly $300 billion just from ads. Together, Big Tech extracts close to half a trillion dollars annually from the global economy.

Where does that money come from?

Big Tech doesn't create that wealth out of nowhere. They sell your attention to the world's largest corporations.

The grocery chains, energy providers, and automotive companies you rely on spend billions of your dollars to advertise on these platforms. Those massive marketing budgets aren't absorbed by the companies—they are baked directly into the prices you pay at the register and on your monthly statements.

The Hidden Tax on Your Life

When you buy food, pay your electricity bill, or finance your car, a hidden fraction of that payment is funneled directly into Silicon Valley's ad networks.

You are indirectly funding the very platforms that harvest your data, manipulate your attention, and fracture society. Your essential bills are subsidizing Big Tech's record-breaking profits.
