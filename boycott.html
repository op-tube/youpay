The Boycott List

You've seen how your everyday bills fund Big Tech. Here's exactly who's charging you for the privilege — and who isn't. Sixteen of the UK's biggest energy, grocery and pub brands, audited against the Meta Ad Library and the Google Ads Transparency Center.

£42.6bn

Spent on advertising in the UK in 2024. At least half of it went straight to Google and Meta, according to Press Gazette estimates.

£35.5bn

The UK's digital ad market in 2024 — growing 13% a year, roughly 12 times faster than the UK economy (IAB UK / PwC).

10 of 16

Brands in this audit advertise on both Meta and Google. Exactly one advertises on neither.

Where to take your money instead

You still need energy and food, and you might want a pint from time to time. Here's how to spend without funding the ad machine — or at least funding it as little as the UK market currently allows.

Energy

Ecotricity

The only energy supplier in our audit that doesn't advertise on Facebook or Instagram. Ethical Consumer rates it a Best Buy, its gas is certified vegan, and it builds new wind and solar farms rather than just reselling certificates.

Not spotless — it still buys Google ads — so when you switch, tell them to drop those too.

Household essentials

Home Bargains

The only name on the entire list that advertises on neither Meta nor Google. The family-owned Liverpool discounter (TJ Morris) has grown to hundreds of stores on a prices-not-promises model — and the empty ad libraries are the proof.

Pubs

JD Wetherspoon

Quit social media entirely in April 2018, deleting its accounts across Facebook, Twitter and Instagram over concerns about trolling and data misuse — and never came back. It still runs Google ads, which is the remaining gap in an otherwise rare record.

Toiletries & gifts

Lush

Not in the original audit, but impossible to leave off this list. In November 2021 the Dorset-founded brand switched off Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and Snapchat in all 48 countries where it trades — "until the platforms take action to provide a safer environment for users." Proof that a major UK retailer can walk away and keep growing. They also don't advertise on Google as far as we can tell.

Everything else

Your local independents

Greengrocers, butchers, bakers, markets, refill shops. The closer your money stays to your front door, the less of it gets funnelled into an ad auction run in California. No loyalty app, no data harvest, no sponsored posts.

Make them listen

Boycotts only work when companies know they're being boycotted. Silence reads as indifference.

Email them

Companies track why customers leave far more closely than why they arrive. Use the template below.

2

Energy takes about 15 minutes to move. Supermarkets take one changed walk. Nothing speaks like lost revenue.

3

Tell everyone why

Share the list. A boycott of one is invisible; a boycott of a street is a boardroom agenda item.

Copy-paste complaint template

Subject: I'm leaving because of your ad spend

Dear [Company],

I've learned that [Company] advertises on [Meta / Google / both]. That
budget is built into the prices I pay you, and it funds platforms that
profit from surveillance and attention manipulation.

I'm switching to a competitor that doesn't. If you pull your advertising
from these platforms, I'll happily come back.

[Your name]
